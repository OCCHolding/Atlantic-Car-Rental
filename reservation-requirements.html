Before You Book

Reservation Requirements

What is the minimum age to rent?

You must be at least 23 years old with a valid driver's license.

What documents do I need at pickup?

A valid driver's license and your passport or government-issued ID are required. If your driver's license is not written in Latin script, an International Driving Permit (IDP) is required. A $200 security deposit is also required and may be provided by major credit card or cash.

What is the deposit amount?

A $200 security deposit is required. The deposit may be provided by major credit card or cash and is released or returned after the vehicle is returned in accordance with the rental agreement.

Do you deliver to my hotel or villa?

Yes. We deliver anywhere on the island — airport, hotel, villa, cruise port. Simply tell us your address or hotel name in your reservation request.

Is mileage really unlimited?

Yes. Every vehicle includes unlimited mileage. Drive the entire island as many times as you like.

What if I have an accident or breakdown?

Call us directly at +297 593 1982 or WhatsApp anytime — 24/7. For emergencies involving injury, dial 100 for police or 911 for ambulance, then call us.

Can I take the car off-road?

Only with our Jeep 4x4. Regular cars are not insured for unpaved roads, Arikok National Park, or the rugged north coast. Driving a non-4x4 off-road voids your insurance — see what's covered and excluded.

Can I smoke in the car?

No. Atlantic vehicles are strictly non-smoking. A $250 cleaning fee applies if smoking is detected.

What about fuel?

You receive the car with a full tank. Please return it full. If returned with less fuel, we will refuel and add the cost plus a $15 refuelling service fee.

Is there a cancellation fee?

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before pickup are free. Within 48 hours, a $25 administrative fee may apply.

Can children sit in the front?

No. Children under 5 must be seated in the rear with an appropriate child seat. Infant and child seats are available at $5/day each — just request them at booking.

Do you accept cash?

Yes. We accept cash and major credit cards. Rental payments may also be made by approved bank transfer or supported cryptocurrency where arranged in advance. The $200 security deposit may be provided by cash or major credit card.
