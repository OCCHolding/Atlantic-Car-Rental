3. Who May Drive

3.1 Minimum age: 23 years.

3.2 Every driver must present at the time of rental:

  • A valid driver’s licence
  • A valid passport or government-issued identification
  • An International Driving Permit where the licence is not written in Latin script

3.3 Only drivers named on the signed rental agreement may drive the vehicle. Additional drivers must be present at the time of rental, meet the same requirements, and be named on the agreement. Additional driver: $5 per day.

3.4 Allowing anyone not named on the agreement to drive ends all insurance cover immediately and makes you responsible for the full value of any loss or damage.

4. Security Deposit

4.1 A security deposit of USD $200 is required.

4.2 It may be paid by major credit card or in cash.

4.3 It is released or returned after the vehicle is returned and inspected.

4.4 We may deduct from it any amount you owe under these Terms — damage not covered by insurance, missing fuel, cleaning, traffic fines, late return, loss of use, or claim handling.

4.5 Where what you owe exceeds the deposit, you remain responsible for the balance.

5. Payment

5.1 All charges are in United States dollars.

5.2 We accept cash and major credit cards. Payment by approved bank transfer or supported cryptocurrency can be arranged in advance.

5.3 Foreign cards may incur a conversion fee from your own bank. That fee is not ours and we cannot refund it.

5.4 Rental charges and the deposit are payable at delivery.

5.5 Amounts unpaid when due carry interest of 1% per month.

5.6 Where we have to take steps to recover what you owe, you are responsible for the reasonable costs of doing so.

6. Cancellation and Changes

6.1 Cancel more than 48 hours before pickup: free.

6.2 Cancel within 48 hours: an administrative fee of USD $25 may apply.

6.3 Changes to a confirmed reservation are subject to availability.

6.4 If you do not collect the vehicle at the agreed time and do not contact us, we may release the reservation after two (2) hours and treat it as a late cancellation.

7. Delivery and Return

7.1 We deliver anywhere on Aruba at no charge — airport, hotel, villa, or agreed location.

7.2 You must be present at the agreed place and time with all required documents.

7.3 At delivery we record together the vehicle’s condition, fuel level, and mileage. Raise any discrepancy then, not later.

7.4 Return the vehicle at the agreed date, time, and place, in the same condition and with the same fuel level, ordinary wear excepted.

7.5 If you return the vehicle outside our operating hours, you remain responsible for it until we inspect it when we next open.

7.6 You are responsible for returning the vehicle complete — keys, documents, spare tyre, tools, child seats, and any other equipment supplied. Missing items are charged at replacement cost.

8. Using the Vehicle

8.1 All rentals include unlimited mileage.

8.2 You must drive in accordance with Aruban traffic law. In Aruba, we drive on the right.

8.3 The vehicle may not be used:

  • By anyone not named on the rental agreement, or without a valid licence
  • By anyone under the influence of alcohol or drugs, prescription or otherwise, affecting the ability to drive
  • For any illegal purpose
  • To carry passengers or goods for hire or reward
  • To push or tow anything
  • In any race, contest, rally, or driving lesson
  • Outside the island of Aruba
  • To carry more people than the vehicle is registered to seat
  • Where it is obvious that continuing to drive would damage the vehicle

8.4 Unpaved roads and Arikok
Four-wheel drive is recommended for exploring Arikok National Park. The route to the Natural Pool (Conchi) requires four-wheel drive and is not accessible by conventional car.

Damage from driving a vehicle on terrain it is not suited to is your full responsibility and is not covered by any insurance or waiver.

8.5 You are responsible for the vehicle throughout the rental, including while parked. Lock it and keep the keys secure when it is unattended.

8.6 You must check and maintain fluid levels and tyre pressure during longer rentals, and stop driving if a warning light appears.

8.7 No repairs, servicing, or replacement of parts without our prior written approval.

9. Smoking, Pets and Cleaning

9.1 Smoking is not permitted in any Atlantic vehicle. A cleaning fee of USD $250 applies where there is evidence of smoking.

9.2 Pets are not permitted without prior arrangement. Where a pet has been carried without arrangement, a cleaning fee of up to USD $100 may apply.

9.3 Vehicles returned needing cleaning beyond normal use — sand, spillage, heavy soiling — may incur a cleaning charge of up to USD $100.

10. Fuel

10.1 The vehicle is supplied at a recorded fuel level and must be returned at that level.

10.2 Returned with less fuel: the missing fuel is charged, plus a refuelling service fee of USD $15.

12. Damage, Accidents and Breakdown

12.1 You are responsible for damage to, loss of, or theft of the vehicle during the rental, except to the extent covered by protection you have taken.

12.2 In an accident you must:

  • Make sure everyone is safe
  • Call the police and obtain a police report
  • Notify Atlantic immediately on +297 593 1982
  • Not admit liability or settle with anyone
  • Record the other party’s details and those of any witnesses

12.3 Failure to obtain a police report where one is required may end insurance cover and leave you fully responsible.

12.4 Report theft or vandalism to us and the police as soon as you discover it.

12.5 Loss of use. Where a vehicle is off the road for repair or replacement because of damage you are responsible for, you are also responsible for our loss of use of that vehicle — calculated as the daily rental rate for the class, for the period the vehicle is unavailable, up to a maximum of 14 days.

12.6 In a breakdown, contact us immediately. Do not arrange repairs yourself without our written approval.

12.7 Where a breakdown is caused by mechanical failure not attributable to you, we will provide a replacement vehicle as soon as reasonably possible, subject to availability.

12.8 Lost keys — including electronic keys, remotes and reprogramming — are charged at USD $150, unless you hold Premium protection.

13. Traffic Fines

13.1 You are responsible for all traffic fines, penalties, tolls, and related costs incurred during the rental.

13.2 Where a fine reaches us after the rental has ended, an administrative handling fee of USD $25 applies in addition to the fine.

14. Late Return

14.1 Grace period: one (1) hour.

14.2 Beyond that, a full additional rental day is charged for each day or part-day.

14.3 Not returning the vehicle without contacting us may be treated as unauthorised use and reported to the authorities.
