Exclusions

What Is Never Covered

Hit & Run Incidents

Damage from a hit-and-run — where the other party cannot be identified — is not covered under any plan.

One-Way Accidents into Fixed Objects

Single-vehicle collisions into a fixed object — a pole, wall, rock, or tree — are not covered.

Incidents Inside Arikok National Park

Damage sustained while driving inside Arikok National Park is not covered. Regular vehicles are not insured for unpaved park roads — ask us about our Jeep 4x4 for park access. See reservation requirements for details.
